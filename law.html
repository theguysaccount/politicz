Sources & method

Follow the sources, read the exceptions, and check the current meeting instructions. This redesign retains the original directory; it is not a new legal review.

Open-meetings law, 50 states + DCExisting research: statute name, citation, regular-meeting notice period and public-comment summary, compiled from each state's own code and the Reporters Committee Open Government Guide, then corrected against statute text where the two disagreed. Notice periods are for regular meetings of local bodies; 19 jurisdictions set an open standard ("reasonable notice") rather than a number, and are recorded as such rather than guessed. rcfp.org/open-government-guide
Municipal meeting calendars, 125 largest US cities + 10 countiesThe original directory records these calendar links as fetched and content-checked. That describes the original collection, not an automatic check on each visit. Use the official calendar for current dates and agendas. webapi.legistar.com
Address → jurisdictionUS Census Bureau Geocoder, Public_AR_Current benchmark. Returns state, county, incorporated place, congressional district and both state legislative districts. Federal, no key, authoritative. geocoding.geo.census.gov
Who holds your seats5 Calls (501(c)(4), non-profit) representative lookup — US House, both Senate seats, governor, attorney general, secretary of state and both state legislative chambers, with office phone numbers. 5calls.org
Map geometryUS Census TIGER state boundaries via us-atlas, Albers USA projection, simplified to 0.7px in screen space. github.com/topojson/us-atlas
Attending and participatingOpen-meetings access and public participation can have different legal requirements. Check the local body’s rules and the state-specific sources. Reporters Committee: participation rules by state
Etymologyidiōtēs: Liddell–Scott–Jones, "private person, individual, one in a private station"; the pejorative sense enters English through Latin idiota. ta politiká, polis, politēs: standard. "Love is that which enables choice" is Forrest Landry's formulation. Madison is Federalist 10.
