The idiot test.

Not an insult, and not a quiz about the news. Idiōtēs was the ordinary Athenian word for a private person — someone who left the common business to whoever showed up. It was a description of a choice, not an intelligence. Six questions, computed from your own address and checked against live public records, decide whether you currently hold the job.
