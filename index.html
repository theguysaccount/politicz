03 / Make your move

You don’t have to do everything.
Start with one thing.

You don’t need to be an expert to begin. Pick something you care about, make a little time, and take it from there.

1

Find something that matters to you.

Open your council’s calendar and read the agenda. Look for a decision that touches your street, your neighborhood, or someone you care about.

2

Show up. Listening counts.

Attend a meeting or watch a recording. See how decisions get made. Your first time can simply be about getting your bearings.

3

Bring one useful thing to the room.

A question. A specific experience. An idea. Check the comment rules and sign-up deadline, or ask the clerk how to submit it in writing.

Why Politicz?

Less on the sidelines.
More at the table.

Politics can feel like something happening somewhere else, between people you’ll never meet. But it also happens in your neighborhood, over decisions you can understand and help shape.

Politicz connects a bigger belief to a practical beginning: the future of government should make it easier for ordinary people to be heard. Today, that starts with finding your room.
